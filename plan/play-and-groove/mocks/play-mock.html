2–4 · Play from your keyboard, MIDI and ● Rec

On a synth track the letter keys play the piano roll in key: A–; = the next ten scale notes, Q–P = an octave up, Z/X = octave down/up. The keys are shown next to the note names. 🎛 MIDI plugs in a real keyboard (Chrome, Edge, Firefox), and notes outside the key snap to the nearest note in it. ● Rec counts in one bar, then loops the pattern with a click and adds what you play (shown as outlined notes). One undo removes the whole take.

6 · Chance and humanize

Loudness / Chance switch on the step grid. In Chance mode, dragging a step sets how often it plays (dotted outline + %). Back in Loudness mode, steps that don't always play show a 🎲. Humanize (Sound tab) nudges each hit's timing and loudness a little. Each loop pass varies, but exports match the first pass exactly.
